### ⚠⚠ My BusinessWire absence checks have a ~1-day blind spot — 09-01

Found by accident, and it undercuts the method behind every "the PR isn't out yet" claim in this log.

Adobe's PR published **08-31**. The **domain-restricted businesswire.com exact-title search returned
only prior quarters.** The open web search and stocktitan both had it. **Had I run only my own
documented channel, I would have logged ADBE as "advance not out" on the day it published.**

That is not a harmless miss. Absence findings set the **floor** under a date — it is the entire basis
for the claim that Copart's stored 09-03 is wrong. A false negative pushes a date **later**, silently,
with no symptom to notice.

**Standing rule now:** the BW exact-title search is fine as a **positive** channel but is **not
trustworthy as a negative on its most recent ~1 day**. Any absence driving a floor needs a second,
faster-indexing channel — `stocktitan.net/news/<SYM>/` is one fetch — and the cross-check must itself
be shown current (it carried the 08-31 ADBE release), or it is just a second unverified silence.

This is the second *denying-direction* failure on file, after the PVH propagation lag. The pair
generalises: **any intermediary between publisher and reader — mirror, CDN, search index — can be
late, and late always reads as "not issued."**
