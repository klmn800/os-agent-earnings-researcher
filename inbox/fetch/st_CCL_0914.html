Carnival (CCL) Stock News

CCL NYSE
Overview Financials News SEC Filings

Welcome to our dedicated page for Carnival news (Ticker: CCL), a resource for investors and traders seeking the latest updates and insights on Carnival stock.

Carnival Corporation Ltd. reports news about a global cruise operator with a portfolio that includes AIDA Cruises, Carnival Cruise Line, Costa Cruises, Cunard, Holland America Line, P&O Cruises, Princess Cruises and Seabourn. Coverage commonly includes brand itinerary releases, ship and fleet investments, destination programs in regions such as Alaska, Europe and the Caribbean, and workforce or maritime-training initiatives tied to cruise operations.

Company updates also cover capital returns, operating performance announcements and governance changes. In 2026, Carnival completed the unification of its former dual listed company structure and redomiciled to Bermuda, with common shares listed on the New York Stock Exchange under CCL.

CARNIVAL TROPICALE RESERVATIONS NOW OPEN, SETTING SAIL FROM GALVESTON IN 2028

Rhea-AI Summary

Carnival Cruise Line (CCL) has opened reservations for its new Excel-class ship Carnival Tropicale, which will begin sailing from Galveston on April 15, 2028.

The ship, built at MEYER WERFT in Papenburg, will offer four- to eight-day cruises to Carnival’s Paradise Collection destinations and selected Caribbean ports. Features include the multi-deck Sunsation Point family zone, Carnival WaterWorks Ultra water park with six waterslides, a redesigned Fairway 66 mini golf course, Treehouse Adventure Trail, new arcade spaces, and new global dining concepts such as Uku Lei Lei, Fetaccine and Emeril’s Coastal Seafood. Further entertainment details will be announced later.

Cunard's Queen Mary 2 Will Undergo Stunning Refurbishment to Refine An Icon For a New Generation

Rhea-AI Summary

Carnival (CCL) announced that its British luxury brand Cunard will send flagship Queen Mary 2 into dry dock in Brest, France, from March 31 to May 9, 2027 for a major refurbishment.

The project will add 31 new staterooms, including 16 new Cunard Princess Grill Suites and 15 Britannia staterooms, and refresh all existing Grill Suites with new interiors and spa‑inspired bathrooms. Public areas, including the Queens Grill and Princess Grill restaurants, outdoor deck spaces and the ship’s unique at‑sea kennels, will be upgraded, with increased kennel capacity and an expanded outdoor walking area. The Mareel Wellness & Beauty area, retail spaces, the Illuminations planetarium and other cultural and entertainment venues will also be modernized, while preserving the ship’s Art Deco character and Transatlantic liner identity.

Reimagined Ocean Bar Debuts on Oosterdam with Expanded Entertainment

Rhea-AI Summary

Carnival (CCL), via its Holland America Line brand, debuted a reimagined Ocean Bar on Oosterdam as part of the Holland America Evolution fleet-wide refresh.

The redesigned venue features a new stage for expanded live music, a relocated bar, refreshed seating, and Art Deco- and heritage-inspired interiors using fabrics with recycled content. Ocean Bar’s layout is intended to bring guests closer to performances such as Vivace Strings, soloists, pianists and late-night cabaret. Evolution will revitalize all six Vista and Signature Class ships, including Oosterdam and Zuiderdam, with a more than $500 million investment in public spaces, amenities, and additional staterooms and suites. Bookings are now open for Oosterdam and Zuiderdam cruises following refurbishment.

SEABOURN ANNOUNCES 138-DAY "2029 WORLD CRUISE: ICONIC ISLANDS & REMOTE HORIZONS"

Rhea-AI Summary

Seabourn (CCL) opened bookings for its 138-day “2029 World Cruise: Iconic Islands & Remote Horizons” aboard Seabourn Quest, sailing from Miami to Lisbon from January 7 to May 26, 2029.

The voyage covers more than 32,000 nautical miles, visiting 58 destinations in 31 countries and territories, including 32 islands and more than 25 UNESCO World Heritage Sites. The itinerary features rare calls at remote locations such as Bora Bora, Easter Island, Zanzibar, Nuku Hiva, Praslin, Antsiranana, Akaroa and Burnie, plus 20 extended stays and eight overnight calls in ports including Lima, Easter Island, Bora Bora, Bali, Singapore, Sydney, Zanzibar and Cape Town.

Full World Cruise guests receive inclusions such as up to $8,000 in shipboard credit per suite category, round-trip Business Class air, a pre-cruise hotel night in Miami, an included Bali Seabourn Journey, upgraded Wi‑Fi, and additional amenities. Bookings for the full voyage or eligible segments made by September 17, 2026, qualify for a 10% early booking savings.

Carnival Cruise Line and Barclays Launch New Carnival Rewards Mastercard to Accelerate Travel Experience Through Everyday Purchase Power

Rhea-AI Summary

Carnival Cruise Line (NYSE: CCL) and Barclays US Consumer Bank launched the no-annual-fee Carnival Rewards Mastercard, integrated with the Carnival Rewards loyalty program. The card allows members to earn up to 6x points on eligible Carnival purchases (3x from the card plus up to 3x via Carnival Rewards), 2x points on eligible restaurant and grocery store spending, and 1x on other purchases.

New cardmembers can earn 50,000 bonus points after $1,000 in purchases in the first 90 days, plus an annual 2,500‑point bonus after $2,000 in yearly spend. Card use also generates Status Qualifying Stars, supports real-time redemptions toward Carnival experiences, and includes a 6‑month 0% promotional APR on Carnival cruise booking purchases. Existing Barclays Carnival cardmembers will be transitioned to the new product starting in September, with Fun Points converting 1:1 into Carnival Rewards Points.
