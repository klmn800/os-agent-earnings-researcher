Oracle Sets the Date for its First Quarter Fiscal Year 2027 Earnings Announcement

September 2, 2026
  • Download (opens in new window)

Earnings Results to be released on September 10, 2026, After the Close of the Market

AUSTIN, Texas, Sept. 2, 2026 /PRNewswire/ -- Oracle Corporation today announced that its first quarter fiscal year 2027 results will be released on Thursday, September 10th, after the close of the market.  Oracle will host a conference call and live webcast at 4:00 p.m. Central Time to discuss the financial results.  The live webcast will be available on the Oracle Investor Relations website at www.oracle.com/investor.
