Concentrix Reports Second Quarter 2026 Results

Rhea-AI Summary

Concentrix (NASDAQ: CNXC) reported fiscal Q2 2026 revenue of $2.46 billion, up 1.9% year-on-year (0.6% constant currency). GAAP operating income was $95.4 million and net income $55.3 million, with diluted EPS of $0.86. Adjusted free cash flow reached $242.3 million.

The Board declared a $0.36 quarterly dividend payable August 4, 2026. Q3 2026 guidance calls for revenue of $2.465–$2.490 billion and non-GAAP diluted EPS of $2.65–$2.77. Full-year 2026 guidance targets revenue of $9.925–$10.025 billion and non-GAAP EPS of $10.83–$11.18.
