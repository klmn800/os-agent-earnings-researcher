Welcome to our dedicated page for CarMax news (Ticker: KMX), a resource for investors and traders seeking the latest updates and insights on CarMax stock.

CarMax Inc. reports developments tied to its used-vehicle retail business, wholesale auctions, vehicle purchasing, extended protection products, repair activity and CarMax Auto Finance. Company updates commonly cover retail and wholesale unit trends, comparable-store sales, gross profit per unit, SG&A spending, advertising, restructuring actions and the availability and cost of capital for auto-loan financing.

CarMax news also includes digital and omnichannel car-shopping initiatives, including online buying and selling tools, as well as governance updates such as board composition, executive leadership changes and shareholder engagement. These developments reflect the company’s model of combining a national store base, online capabilities, vehicle appraisal and financing services for used-car buyers and sellers.

Gen Alpha Turns 16: New CarMax Survey Reveals the Next Generation of Drivers Is Ready to Get Behind the Wheel

Rhea-AI Summary

CarMax (NYSE: KMX) released survey findings on Gen Alpha teens as the first of this generation turn 16 and approach getting a driver’s license. According to CarMax, 80% of Gen Alpha teens ages 14–16 already have or plan to get their license as soon as legally allowed, and 68% are excited about driving. Three-quarters view a license as a major life milestone, driven by desires for independence and less reliance on parents.

The survey shows 95% of these teens expect immediate access to a vehicle once licensed, with 40% expecting their family to purchase a car for them. Despite being digital natives, 86% believe it is important to see, sit in and test drive a vehicle before purchase. CarMax highlights its omnichannel model, with online research tools, tens of thousands of vehicles including over 25,000 priced under $25,000, no-haggle pricing, CarMax Certified quality, a 10-Day Money-Back Guarantee and a 30-day limited warranty to support families buying a first car.

CarMax Teams with Sierra to Enhance Inbound Sales Call Experience

Rhea-AI Summary

CarMax (NYSE: KMX) is working with Sierra to enhance its inbound sales call experience using Sierra’s AI, voice-enabled agents, deployed in May 2026. The agents route callers to the right destination or associate more quickly and consistently, ask clarifying questions, answer common queries like store hours and vehicle availability, and hand off to CarMax associates when needed.

According to CarMax, since launch it has seen higher call resolution and fewer unresolved calls, and it plans to add appointment-management features. The company notes this partnership extends its longstanding use of AI, including its Skye virtual assistant and a car-shopping app in the ChatGPT app store.
