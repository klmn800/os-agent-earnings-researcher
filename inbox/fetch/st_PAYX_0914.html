WISE at Work: Early Results Show Paychex AI Is Reducing Payroll Errors and Improving Service Efficiency at Scale

Rhea-AI Summary

Paychex (Nasdaq: PAYX) reported early results from more than 50,000 businesses using its WISE (Workforce Intelligence, Strengthened by Expertise) AI platform, showing gains in payroll accuracy, service speed, and operational efficiency. According to the company, WISE’s Intelligent Pay Cycle helped correct nearly 90% of identified time and pay rate errors before payroll run day, while average time to review and resolve direct deposit changes fell from about three days to under two days.

WISE voice and email agents have handled over 350,000 workforce conversations, resolving more than 20% without human involvement. The payroll voice agent is operating at nearly 100% accuracy with over 40,000 regular users, and the email agent processes nearly 300,000 interactions at an average of three minutes, which Paychex reports is more than 80% faster than human handling. The company is expanding WISE capabilities across Paychex Flex and internal sales, service, and implementation workflows.

Paychex (NASDAQ: PAYX) reports that its July Small Business Employment Watch shows the U.S. small business jobs index at 99.23, essentially steady and slightly above the 99.20 average for the first half of 2026 for firms with fewer than 50 employees.
